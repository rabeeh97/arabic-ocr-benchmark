## Reading notes

- Coverage is near-saturated (96-99 % known), so for decoding the binding number is the false-accept table: the common union accepts at least ~23 % of single dot errors (the max over its members), so a lexicon constraint alone cannot restore dots; frequency (Wikipedia counts) or an LM has to rank candidates.
- Ayaspell and Pecina enforce hamza spelling; Muharaf and RASAM texts often omit it, which shows up as their large variant-only share (12-16 %) against aspell-ar's ~1.5 %.
- Name sources contain many common words (organisation and place labels), so their own coverage is not a name rate; the name share of OOV (column 7 of the first table) is the relevant figure.
- Affix lexicons (Ayaspell, aspell-ar) are tested with `scripts/lexicon/affix.py` (`Checker`), which accepts exactly what the expander generates (checked on 107k forms of 300 random Ayaspell stems: 0 misses).
