Dataset geometry transfer

Generated by scripts/geom_transfer.py and scripts/build_transfer_html.py. Local page, not yet in the ledger.

Each line of one dataset is resized to 64 px high (the CRNN's input) and passed through five steps whose strengths were fitted so that the measured medians of the output match the target dataset: slant (row shear), stroke width (grey-level erosion or dilation, separately for vertical and horizontal strokes), blur or sharpening, ink and paper tone, and paper noise. The text is untouched, so every transformed line keeps its label. Images show the right-most 640 px of each line, since the script runs right to left.

The measured scalars move to the target as intended. The contour classifier moves little, because these steps change how a line is scanned and inked, not the shape of its letters. Curviness and dot-to-stroke ratio stay where they were (see each table). What the transformation therefore tests is how much of a transfer gap is carried by slant, tone and stroke weight, which a model run on the transformed lines can answer.
