Letter-shape library and novelty

Letter-shape novelty against errors (X-G10)

Every letter of 2,000 Muharaf training lines (75,182 letters) forms the training bank. Each test letter is located by CTC forced alignment, described by the CRNN’s CNN features inside its span, and scored by its distance to the nearest training letter of the same character and positional form, relative to the typical distance inside the bank (1 = as typical as a training letter). The line marks whether the model’s greedy reading got that letter right.

In every set the error rate climbs steeply with novelty, and KHATT has twice NAKBA’s share of letters beyond the most unusual 5 % of Muharaf’s own test letters. Within the same band KHATT letters are still misread more often, so novelty is a large part of the gap but not all of it. Caveat: novelty is measured with the same model’s features, so letters it is unsure of may also look unusual to it; a second model trained separately is the planned check.
