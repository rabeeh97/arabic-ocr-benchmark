Models

mock_echo
Not a model — a test stub that returns the ground truth verbatim. Its row is the pipeline's upper bound (CER 0%); if it isn't zero, scoring is broken.
mock_empty
Test stub that returns an empty string. Its row is the pipeline's lower bound (CER ~100%); a "predict nothing" baseline every real model must beat.

Datasets

synthetic
Ten hard-coded Arabic sentences rendered to plain images, shipped in the repo. Not a benchmark — an offline check that the runner, scorer and leaderboard work end to end without any download.
