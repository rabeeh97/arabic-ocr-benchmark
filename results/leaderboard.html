Models

openrouter-qwen3-vl-235b
Qwen3-VL 235B (A22B MoE) via OpenRouter — current-generation large open VLM with very strong multilingual OCR. No local GPU needed.
openrouter-qwen2.5-vl-72b
Qwen2.5-VL 72B via OpenRouter — a large open VLM with strong OCR, no local GPU needed. OpenRouter also exposes many models on one key.
gemini-flash-lite
Google's cheapest/fastest Gemini (3.5 Flash-Lite). Low-cost baseline row.
qari-ocr-0.3
NAMAA-Space's Arabic OCR model, 2B params, fine-tuned from Qwen2-VL-2B. The v0.3 line is "structure-first" — tuned for full-page documents with layout and Markdown output, so on single lines it can add markup or over-generate.
openrouter-llama-4-maverick
Meta Llama 4 Maverick (multimodal MoE) via OpenRouter. A current open baseline; historically weaker on non-Latin script than Qwen-VL.
qwen2.5-vl-3b
Alibaba's Qwen2.5-VL, 3B params. A capable general-purpose vision-language model with notably strong OCR across scripts; no Arabic-specific tuning.
trocr-large-arabic
TrOCR-Large (0.6B, vision-encoder-decoder) finetuned for Arabic + English handwriting on KHATT and IAM. A dedicated HTR model rather than a general VLM — no prompt, single-line only. Runs on CPU/MPS; expect it to do best on KHATT-like lines and worse on out-of-distribution scripts (e.g. Muharaf).
mistral-small
Mistral Small (multimodal). Small, cheap, general-purpose.
qari-ocr-0.2
NAMAA-Space's earlier Arabic OCR checkpoint (v0.2.2.1), a LoRA adapter on Qwen2-VL-2B, tuned for raw-text accuracy on diacritically rich Arabic. Reports the best character accuracy of the Qari line on its own benchmark.
qwen2-vl-2b
Alibaba's Qwen2-VL, 2B params, untuned. The base model the Qari checkpoints are built on — its row shows how much the Arabic OCR fine-tuning actually buys.
pixtral-12b
Mistral's open vision-language model (12B), served via Mistral's API. Free tier available. A useful open-weight comparison point without local GPU.

Datasets

kitab_khatt
KITAB-Bench / KHATT subset — modern handwritten Arabic, line images with clean printed-quality ground truth. The main handwriting target: cleaner and more uniform than Muharaf, so a useful contrast with it.
kitab_muharaf
KITAB-Bench / Muharaf subset — historical handwritten Arabic segments (a small slice of the same Muharaf corpus as `muharaf_lines`), for a direct KITAB-Bench-scored comparison point.
muharaf_lines
Muharaf ("Manuscripts of Handwritten Arabic", NeurIPS 2024), line-level split. Real 19th-to-21st-century handwritten letters, diaries and records — predominantly Ruq'ah script, often degraded, with natural diacritic use. This is the held-out `test` split (1,334 lines); train/validation are reserved for fine-tuning. CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0, research use.
