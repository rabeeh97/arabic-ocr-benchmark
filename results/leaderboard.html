Models

qari-ocr-0.2
NAMAA-Space's earlier Arabic OCR checkpoint (v0.2.2.1), a LoRA adapter on Qwen2-VL-2B, tuned for raw-text accuracy on diacritically rich Arabic. Reports the best character accuracy of the Qari line on its own benchmark.

Datasets

muharaf_lines
Muharaf ("Manuscripts of Handwritten Arabic", NeurIPS 2024), line-level split. Real 19th-to-21st-century handwritten letters, diaries and records — predominantly Ruq'ah script, often degraded, with natural diacritic use. This is the held-out `test` split (1,334 lines); train/validation are reserved for fine-tuning. CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0, research use.
