Where the model looks when it reads

Do the maps work?

Three ways of asking which part of the image produced one generated token. Each is checked three ways: does hiding the highlighted patches wreck that token, does the highlight land on ink, and does it move right to left along the line.

Wrong tokens versus correct tokens

A token counts as wrong when its characters differ from the ground truth after character alignment. The error panel was built from the 15 worst lines of this model plus 8 easy ones, so the share of wrong tokens here is not an error rate.

By letter family

Letter panel only (20 lines chosen for their letters, not their errors). A token can appear in several rows. Error rate is the share of those tokens with a mismatch; counts are small, so read it as a lead, not a result.
